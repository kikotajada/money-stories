Inside the guide

What it looks like

It's for you if…

  • You earn a Swiss salary and your savings sit in a bank account.
  • You're not sure whether to start with 3a, an ETF or the bank's fund.
  • Swiss tax on investments (wealth tax, withholding tax, US vs Irish funds) confuses you.
  • You want to invest a fixed amount every month and stop checking the news.
  • You'd like boring and working over exciting and costly.

It's not for you if…

  • You want us to tell you which fund or broker to buy. This is education, not advice.
  • You want trading, crypto or single stocks to get rich fast.
  • You've been investing in index funds for years.

Questions

Before you buy

How do I get it?

Right after paying you land on your download page: the PDF guide and the calculator (copy it to Google Sheets or download it as Excel). The link is also in your Stripe receipt.

How much money do I need to start?

None to read it. The examples use CHF 500 a month, but every number in the calculator is yours to change.

Do you recommend a broker or a fund?

No. You get the criteria and a calculator to compare on your own plan. We keep a separate, up-to-date broker comparison on money-stories.ch.

Does it work outside Zurich?

Yes. The rules are Swiss; where a canton matters (tax rates, wealth-tax allowances, filing thresholds) the guide says so and the calculator lets you enter your canton's numbers.

What if I'm leaving Switzerland?

The guide covers what happens to your 3a and pension fund when you leave, and why the currency you'll retire in matters.
