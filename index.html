He opened the wrong pillar 3

James wanted to save tax, so he opened a pillar 3. A 3b. Only the 3a is deductible: up to CHF 7,258 a year in 2026 if you have a pension fund. And since 2026, missed 3a years from 2025 onwards can be paid in later.
